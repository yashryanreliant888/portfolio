# Product Context

**Product Name:** Yash Portfolio

**Platform:** web

**Mode:** experience

**Audience:** Design directors, creative recruiters, potential clients, and peers in the design community looking to evaluate portfolio work and creative capabilities.

**Purpose:** Showcase design work in a way that commands attention and reflects confidence, sophistication, and technical craft. The portfolio itself is the first artifact visitors judge — it must demonstrate the same level of excellence expected in the work it contains.

**Voice & Tone:** Confident, expensive, slightly secretive. Like a premium lounge at 2am with a single neon sign. Editorial without being instructive. Warm geometry that feels both clinical and human. The brand speaks through restraint and precision, not volume.

**Core Experience Goals:**
- Let the work lead from the first viewport; the interface recedes
- Create a sense of expensive exclusivity through rationed color and shadowless elevation
- Feel technically extraordinary through precise typography and unexpected details
- Navigate like reading a luxury travel editorial, not clicking through a grid

**Constraints:**
- Signal Violet (#af50ff) appears only 1-2 times per page — treat as runway lighting
- System is 95% achromatic, shadowless by design
- No product screenshots or lifestyle photos after hero — iconographic dominance
- Typography carries all the drama: three fonts doing distinct jobs that never overlap

**Success Metrics:**
- Visitor lingers on work samples rather than hunting for navigation
- Portfolio itself becomes a case study of the designer's capabilities
- Brand is memorable without being loud — visitors remember "the one with the violet glow"
